I'm a senior student of the Licentiate in Information Technology Management (ATI) at the Costa Rica Institute of Technology (TEC), a SINAES-accredited program. The ATI profile trains people who understand both the business and the technology, and that's where I work best: at the border between the two.

Ahora mismo llevo cursos como Administración de Procesos de Negocios, Administración de Tecnologías de Información, Adquisición de TI y Sistemas de Información Empresarial. Son los cursos donde se integra todo lo aprendido en el camino: cómo la tecnología genera valor real para las empresas hoy, no como piezas sueltas sino como un sistema.

Right now I'm taking courses like Business Process Management, IT Management, IT Acquisition and Enterprise Information Systems — the courses where everything I've learned so far comes together: how technology creates real value for a company today, not as loose parts but as a system.

El resto de mi vida —el trabajo, los proyectos propios, el deporte— se apoya en esa base y forma una integración troncal de mi perfil. The rest of my life — work, my own projects, sport — builds on that foundation and forms the backbone of my profile. I work as an administrative assistant, build websites independently, and have played first-division volleyball for 6 years, as well as being captain of my university team.
